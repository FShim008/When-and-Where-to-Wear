BEFORE USING THESE NUMBERS:
 1. Every value in section 1 is a guess. Re-run from pilot estimates.
 2. H3 is the binding constraint, not H1. Do not size the study on the main effect.
 3. Because §3 gates H1 and H2 on H3, an underpowered interaction weakens ALL THREE
    hypotheses, not just the third one.
 4. If N cannot be raised far enough, preregister H3 as underpowered and exploratory
    and say so in the paper. That is honest and publishable; discovering it afterwards
    is neither.
 5. Random slopes are simulated. Dropping them would raise every figure here and be wrong.
