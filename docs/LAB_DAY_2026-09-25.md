### A2. Does `PlayMotors` replace or queue? *(~10 min, same setup)*

`ContinuousCueGate` **assumes replace**. If the SDK queues, overlap accumulates across an approach, the
cue lags the participant's motion, and H4′ measures a delayed stimulus — **while every log still looks
correct**. This is the single most dangerous unknown in the PBC condition.

Put `SessionRunner` in a **PBC** block and move a tracked hand slowly toward an obstacle.

| What you feel | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Buzz strengthens as you close, **stops within ~0.06 s** of pulling away | ✅ replace — assumption holds |
| Buzz **lags** your motion, or keeps buzzing after you pull away | ❌ queue — **tell me, the timing needs inverting** |

- [ ] Verdict: ____________

Then read the console after the block:
```
[SessionRunner] PBC cue delivery: N submitted, M throttled (X% reached the device)
```
- [ ] Submit rate: ______ %   ← **below 25% means the ramp is coarser than designed, tell me**
